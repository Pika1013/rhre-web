RHRE Web 日本語

Make Rhythm Heaven remixes right in your browser

Welcome to RHRE Web! Put Rhythm Heaven sound effects on your favorite songs and make your own remixes, on your phone or PC. It's a free, fan-made editor, and your remixes are compatible with RHRE fresh (PC).

Open RHRE Web No install needed. Choose “English” on the first screen.

What you can do

  • Open and save .rhref filesRemixes open in RHRE fresh as they are. Your work is saved on your device automatically.
  • Plays like RHRE freshSame playback rules: gradual tempo changes, swing, pitch, loops, patterns and more.
  • Every minigamePlace, move, stretch, copy and paste, change pitch and volume, undo and redo.
  • Your own musicAdd songs from your device. Type the tempo, tap along, or let it detect the tempo for you, even when it changes mid-song.
  • Export audio and videoSave your remix as a WAV file, or as an MP4 video.
  • Play modePress buttons along with the cues, with Perfect Challenge and night mode. Presentation mode too.

Getting started

iPhone

Open the page in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Open RHRE Web from your home screen.

Android

Open the page in Chrome and choose “Install app” from the menu. You can also get the Android app (APK) from GitHub Releases.

PC

Just open the page in your browser.

RHRE Web works in landscape only. A short tutorial starts the first time you open it.

RHRE Web is a fan-made project based on RHRE fresh. It is not affiliated with Nintendo.
